# Synthetic Multimodal Information Fusion

This synthetic direction exists only to exercise the public Idea Pipeline. It contains no real research corpus or unpublished experimental claims.

## Common Pitfalls

- Adding visual feature layers does not prove that the model uses more task-relevant evidence.
- A higher aggregate score does not prove that every modality and task category improved.

## Research Opportunities

- Compare early, late and gated fusion with the same model, data and compute budget.
- Report separate results for text-led, vision-led and conflicting inputs.
