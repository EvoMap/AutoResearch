# Synthetic Visual Token Management

This synthetic direction exists only to exercise the public Idea Pipeline. It contains no real research corpus or unpublished experimental claims.

## Common Pitfalls

- Fewer tokens do not prove that the system preserved useful visual evidence.
- Lower mean latency cannot replace checks for worst-case examples and lost critical regions.

## Research Opportunities

- Compare static, query-aware and layer-wise dynamic pruning under a fixed accuracy target.
- Run masking controls on removed tokens and measure whether they change the final answer.
