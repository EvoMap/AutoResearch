# Synthetic Research Direction

## Problem

Describe a research problem without copying confidential or licensed source text.

## Constraints

- State measurable success and failure conditions.
- List available compute and data boundaries.
- Record assumptions that an experiment can disprove.

## Baselines

List public baselines and primary-source links that the user has reviewed.

## 常见误区 / 错误直觉

- List shortcuts or assumptions that prior evidence does not support.

## 可行的创新切入点

- List small experiments that can distinguish competing explanations.
